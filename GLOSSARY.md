# TutorDek

TutorDek connects learners with tutors and provides a place to practice and track learning.

## Language

**Learner**: A student who books tutoring sessions and studies with TutorDek.
_Avoid_: Customer

**Tutor**: A subject specialist whom a learner can choose for a tutoring session.
_Avoid_: Robot Tutor when referring to a person

**Booking**: A learner's reservation of one tutor for a one-hour session, with a subject, topic, time, and learning mode.
_Avoid_: Subscription

**Study package**: A collection of lessons and practice activities that a learner can add to their learning plan.
_Avoid_: Booking

**Learning assistant**: A helper that retrieves explanations from TutorDek's learning materials.
_Avoid_: Human tutor

**BrainBoost**: A short practice exercise with feedback and a recorded result.

**Study room**: The workspace for a booked session, with session details, notes, and a drawing board.
